🎱 Pool Aim Lens

Point your phone at a pool game on another screen. The app draws where the cue ball and the ball it hits will go.
  1. Live camera, or snap a photo / video of the game.
  2. Tap Set table, then tap the 4 inner corners of the felt, going around. Start on a long edge.
  3. Line up a shot in the game. The app tracks the cue stick. You can also drag on the screen to aim yourself.
  4. If balls aren't picked up, change Ball size and Sensitivity. Debug shows what the app sees.
